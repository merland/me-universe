# Glossary

My own shorthand and names for things. One line per term, each dated with its origin.

- **me-universe** — this repository, my personal knowledge base (2026-10-09, me).
- **pw: name** — a pointer to an entry in my password manager, which is LastPass; the wiki never holds the secret itself. I have not decided a naming convention for entries yet (2026-10-09, me).
