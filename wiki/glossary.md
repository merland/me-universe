# Glossary

My own shorthand and names for things. One line per term, each dated with its origin.

- **me-universe** — this repository, my personal knowledge base (2026-10-09, me).
- **pw: name** — a pointer to an entry in my password manager, which is LastPass; the wiki never holds the secret itself. I have not decided a naming convention for entries yet (2026-10-09, me).
- **LT1** — the aerobic threshold, what my 2019 lab report calls "Aerob Tröskel (LT), FatMax"; see [running-physiology](running-physiology.md) (2026-10-09, me).
- **LT2** — the anaerobic threshold or lactate threshold heart rate, what my 2019 lab report calls "Anaerob tröskel (AT)"; see [running-physiology](running-physiology.md) (2026-10-09, me).
- **engine vs chassis** — my analogy for cardiovascular capacity versus the legs' tolerance to prolonged loading; an analogy, not a diagnosis (2026-10-09, me).
