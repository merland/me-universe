# Suunto daily summary

Monthly figures from the daily data under `sources/suunto/`, printed by `scripts/suunto-summary.py` and pasted here; regenerate rather than edit. How the data gets here and what each era can and cannot say is on [suunto-export](suunto-export.md).

Reading the columns: a night is the sleep fragments keyed to the evening they started; nights summing past 16 hours are export artefacts and are left out. Steps per day counts only days the watch sampled most of the day. HR days are days with near-full wrist coverage; Rest HR is the median over those days of the lowest ten-minute average, a proxy, not a measured resting heart rate; sleep HR and HRV are the Run 2's own nightly values. All heart rates are in bpm, converted from the export's beats per second. Spartan Ultra sleep, 2018 to 2023, is movement-based with no heart rate. The heart-rate table stays empty until the export of 2026-11 files October 2026, the first month with wrist data (2026-10-22, session).
