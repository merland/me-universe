# erlandsson.se

A domain I manage. It is registered with AWS Route 53, and its DNS is hosted there too (2026-10-09, me). Email for the domain is handled by Google Workspace (2026-10-09, me). My main Google Workspace account is martin@erlandsson.se (2026-10-09, me). A catch-all delivers mail to every other address on the domain to that account; mail to any address on [megabit-se](megabit-se.md) is delivered there as well (2026-10-09, me). Where the catch-all is configured is unknown; I did not record it (2026-10-09, me).

Outgoing mail is authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC at policy `p=none`, with end-to-end verification still open; details are on [email-authentication](email-authentication.md) (2026-10-09, me).

Password-manager entries for the AWS account and the Google Workspace admin: unknown. Other uses of the domain beyond email: unknown.
