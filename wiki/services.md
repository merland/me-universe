# Online services

The services I use or pay for, and what each is for. This list is incomplete on purpose; I named these first and stopped (2026-10-09, me).

- **Google Workspace** — email for [megabit-se](megabit-se.md) and [erlandsson-se](erlandsson-se.md); the main account is martin@erlandsson.se; catch-all routing for both domains is configured here (2026-10-09, me).
- **GitHub** — one account, merland, used for both personal and work repositories; it is also linked to my work address martin.erlandsson@piffl.se (2026-10-10, me).
- **Airtable** — experiments, plus a base called "My forms" that collects the submissions from the contact forms on my domains (2026-10-10, me).
- **AWS** — Route 53 is the registrar and DNS host for megabit.se and erlandsson.se, and for a few more domains held mainly for possible future use, whose names I keep out of the wiki for now (2026-10-10, me). Lightsail hosts the placeholder websites, simple containers deployed by AWS; most of my domains host only a contact form (2026-10-10, me).
- **Strava** — logs my runs and shows race-time predictions; see [running-races](running-races.md) (2026-10-09, me).
- **LastPass** — my password manager; see `pw:` in the [glossary](glossary.md) (2026-10-09, me).
- **Kivra** — the mailbox of my company [megabit-rd-ab](megabit-rd-ab.md), which also receives ordinary email (2026-10-10, me). Kivra once rejected mail from megabit.se, see [email-authentication](email-authentication.md).
- **Gmail** — my old address marterland@gmail.com still receives but forwards everything to martin@erlandsson.se (2026-10-10, me).

Plan, cost and renewal date for each: unknown.
